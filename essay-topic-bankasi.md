# Akademik Essay Topic Bankası — Gerçek Sınav Verilerine Dayalı

Bu liste, **IELTS Writing Task 2 (2024-2025) gerçek sınav raporlarından** ve Türk üniversitelerinin Erasmus İngilizce yeterlilik sınavlarının IELTS/YDS benzeri yapısından derlenmiştir. Topic seçerken **gerçekten sınanma olasılığı yüksek** konulara öncelik ver.

**Önemli not:** Erasmus sınavı üniversiteden üniversiteye değişir, ama topic havuzu genellikle bu listeyle %80+ örtüşür. Aşağıdaki tema dağılımı son 2 yılın IELTS verisine bağlı.

## 🆕 SON YILLARDA GERÇEKTEN ÇIKMIŞ SORULAR (IELTS 2024-2025-2026)

Bunlar **uydurma değil** — gerçekten test edilmiş sorular. Yapın bunlardan rastgele 1 tane verip "şunu yaz" demek.

### Eğitim
- *"Some people believe that children should start school at a very early age. Others think they should begin later. Discuss both views and give your opinion."* (Sık tekrarlayan)
- *"University students should be required to attend classes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"*
- *"Some people think standardized tests are the fairest way to measure student ability. To what extent do you agree?"*
- *"Some people believe that only the most outstanding students should be rewarded. Others think it is more important to reward students who show improvements. Discuss both views and give your opinion."* (Eki 2025)
- *"Some people think it is more effective for students to study in a group, while others believe that they should study alone. Discuss both views and give your opinion."* (Kasım 2025)
- *"In many countries, fewer students choose science subjects at university. What problems does this cause and what solutions can be suggested?"*
- *"Some people believe that students should begin learning a language very early in school while others think these subjects should be taught later. Discuss both sides and give your own opinion."*
- *"Many today feel that attention spans are becoming shorter due to the prevalence of social media. To what extent do you agree?"*

### Teknoloji & AI
- *"Artificial intelligence will soon replace teachers. Do you agree or disagree?"*
- *"Some people think that the internet has brought people closer together, while others believe it has made them more isolated. Discuss both views and give your opinion."*
- *"Some people think that modern communication technology is having a negative effect on social relationships. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"* (Şubat 2025)
- *"It will be better to have wide use of driverless cars for individuals and society. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"*
- *"Companies regularly introduce new products and encourage consumers to replace their old models. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?"* (Aralık 2025)
- *"Using computer every day can have more negatives than positives effects on young children. Do you agree or disagree?"*
- *"New technologies have changed the way children spend their free time. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?"*

### Çevre & İklim
- *"Some believe that tackling environmental issues should be managed by a global organization instead of national governments. Discuss both views and give your opinion."*
- *"Some people believe that in the future, most cities will become unlivable due to pollution and overcrowding. To what extent do you agree?"*
- *"What causes environmental damage, and what should be done about it?"*
- *"Some people feel that it is impossible for a country to be economically progressive and environmentally friendly at the same time. Discuss."*
- *"What are the challenges of adopting a sustainable lifestyle? What actions can individuals take to contribute to sustainability?"*

### Sağlık
- *"Some people think that the government should subsidise fruits and vegetables to make healthy food more affordable. Others argue that the government should tax unhealthy food instead. Discuss both views and give your opinion."*
- *"A growing number of people with health problems are trying alternative medicine instead of visiting their usual doctor. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?"*
- *"Healthcare costs are increasing. Should citizens be totally responsible for their own health with private health insurance or should the government provide free healthcare for all? Discuss."*
- *"Doctors recommend that older people exercise regularly. However, many of them do not get enough exercise. What are the reasons and what can be done?"*

### İş & Kariyer
- *"Remote work has become increasingly common in recent years. Some argue this impairs work habits. To what extent do you agree?"*
- *"Many believe that the goal of one's career should be to pursue a passion while others feel it is merely a way to earn a livelihood. Discuss both sides and give your own opinion."*
- *"Some people think that the ability to compete with others for a successful career is more important. Others believe that the ability to work as part of a team is more important. Discuss."* (Kasım 2025)
- *"Some feel that individuals should have the right to strike in all jobs while others feel there are exceptions."*

### Toplum & Aile
- *"Today, many people do not know their neighbors. What are the reasons and what are the consequences?"* (Mart 2025)
- *"Some people prefer to spend their lives doing the same things and avoiding change. Others think that change is always a good thing. Discuss."* (Mart 2025)
- *"Some people think that parents should teach their children how to be good members of society. Others believe that school is the best place to learn this. Discuss both views."*
- *"In many countries, people increasingly talk about money in their daily conversations. Is this a positive or negative development?"* (Şubat 2025)

### Government Spending
- *"Some people say that more money should be invested in science than in other subjects. To what extent do you agree?"*
- *"Some governments cannot afford to spend money on art. Should governments fund museums and theaters with public money?"*

### Medya & Kültür
- *"Some people today argue that media has become increasingly nostalgic and unoriginal. Discuss."* (Aralık 2025)
- *"Some think newspapers are the best method for reading the news while others think other media is better."*
- *"Some feel that movies and TV shows are a good way to study history despite their lack of historical accuracy at times. Discuss."*
- *"Many think that religion should be taught in schools while others think it should be avoided. Discuss both sides."* (Mayıs 2025)
- *"Some people feel that cities should allow for spaces for graffiti while others feel it should be banned."* (Şubat 2025)

### Travel & Tourism
- *"More and more people are traveling to untouched, unexplored natural places. What are the advantages and disadvantages for individuals and the country?"* (Aralık 2025)
- *"Does tourism endanger culture, or is it beneficial? Discuss both sides and give your opinion."*
- *"Some people prefer staying in hotels when they travel, while others prefer renting holiday homes for a short period of time. Discuss."*

### Sport
- *"To be successful in sport, some people believe it is essential to have a good coach. Others say that an athlete's natural ability is more important. Discuss."* (Aralık 2025)
- *"Are professional athletes positive role models, or can their behavior negatively influence young people? Discuss both views."*
- *"Why are fewer young people playing sports today? What can be done to encourage participation?"*

## TOPIC SEÇME PROTOKOLÜ (Skill için)

Kullanıcıya topic verirken:

1. **Önce tema seç** — 10 tema arasından rotasyonla (en son hangi temayı verdiysen, ondan en az 2 hafta uzaklaş)
2. **Sonra topic seç** — o temada yukarıdaki gerçek sorulardan birini al
3. **Essay türünü** kullanıcının çalışması gereken alana göre belirle (agree/disagree zayıfsa onu ver)
4. **Verirken şu format kullan:**

```
**Topic (Tür: Agree/Disagree, Tema: Eğitim):**

"Artificial intelligence will soon replace teachers. Do you agree or disagree?"

**Notlar:**
- Süre: 60 dk (akademik kalemle yaz, sonra dijital)
- Kelime: 300-500
- Bu topic son yıllarda IELTS'te birkaç kez çıktı, sınav formatına uygun
- Hem teknoloji hem eğitim teması — iki kategorinin de kelime hazinesini gözden geçir

**Sana yardımcı 3 fikir noktası (kullanman zorunlu değil, sadece düşünmen için):**
- AI tutoring'in bireyselleştirilmiş eğitim potansiyeli
- İnsan öğretmenin empati ve adapte olma gücü
- Hibrit model olabilirliği

Yazdığında bana gönder, rubriğe göre değerlendireyim.
```

## TEMA-TÜR EŞLEŞTİRME REHBERİ

Her tema farklı essay türlerine uygun. Aşağıdaki tablo öğrencinin çalışmak istediği türe göre topic önermeyi kolaylaştırır:

| Tür | En uygun temalar | Örnek topic |
|-----|------------------|-------------|
| **Agree/Disagree** | Teknoloji (AI replace), Eğitim (free uni), Hükümet (voting) | "AI will replace teachers. Agree or disagree?" |
| **Discuss Both Views** | Eğitim (early school), Toplum (change vs stability), İş (passion vs livelihood) | "Children: early school start vs later. Discuss." |
| **Problem-Solution** | Çevre, Sağlık (mental health), Toplum (neighbors), Eğitim (declining science) | "Mental health crisis in young adults. Problems and solutions?" |
| **Advantages-Disadvantages** | Travel (untouched nature), Tech (children + tech), İş (remote work) | "Untouched nature tourism. Adv vs disadv?" |
| **Opinion** | Government spending, Medya, Kültür, Karma temalar | "Money invested in science vs arts. Opinion?" |

## ROTASYON ÖRNEKLERİ (4 Haftalık Döngü)

Aynı temayı 2 hafta üst üste verme. İdeal 4 haftalık ritim:

**Döngü A (sınavda en çok çıkanlara odak):**
- Hafta 1: Eğitim — Agree/Disagree
- Hafta 2: Teknoloji & AI — Discuss Both Views
- Hafta 3: Çevre & İklim — Problem-Solution
- Hafta 4: Sağlık — Discuss Both Views

**Döngü B (çeşitlilik):**
- Hafta 1: İş & Kariyer — Discuss Both Views
- Hafta 2: Toplum & Aile — Problem-Solution
- Hafta 3: Medya & Kültür — Agree/Disagree
- Hafta 4: Travel — Advantages-Disadvantages

**Döngü C (sınav öncesi son ay, Faz 3):**
- Tema seçimine bakma — **rastgele** son yıllarda çıkmış soruyu çek, sınav simülasyonu yap
